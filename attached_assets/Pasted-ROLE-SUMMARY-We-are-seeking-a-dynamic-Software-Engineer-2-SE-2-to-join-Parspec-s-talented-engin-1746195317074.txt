engineering team. This pivotal role requires a blend of backend system design, coding
expertise, project leadership, cross-functional collaboration, and mentoring. As an SE-2, you
will drive the delivery of innovative features, ensuring robustness, scalability, and seamless
integration with Parspec's AI-powered platform.

You will collaborate closely with the Product, Frontend, and AI teams to transform feature
ideas into reliable solutions, playing an active role in the product lifecycle from estimation
to production deployment & its associated support.

design, and adherence to best practices.

infrastructure on AWS/GCP.

challenges in production systems.
